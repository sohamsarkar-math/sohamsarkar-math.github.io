* Developed predictive models in R using multiple linear and logistic regression to identify heart failure risk from clinical data
* Conducted feature analysis to rank clinically important risk factors and quantify observations through machine learning tools
* Successfully achieved high classification accuracy, demonstrating the model's reliability in forecasting mortality events from electronic health records
