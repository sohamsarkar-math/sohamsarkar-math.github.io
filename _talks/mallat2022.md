Presented at [Image and Signal Analysis Journal Club](https://math.utdallas.edu/archive-ay-23-image-and-signal-analysis-journal-club/)
* Discussed Mallat’s Theory, focusing on the mathematical framework of multiresolution signal decomposition via orthonormal wavelet bases
* Evaluated the implementation of wavelet representation in the contexts of image coding, texture discrimination, and fractal analysis
