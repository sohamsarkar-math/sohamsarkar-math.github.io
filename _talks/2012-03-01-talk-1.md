* Developed MATLAB based optimization model in lumped laser model with SESAM with order 1 accuracy
* Linearized pulse to determine stability using a finite difference scheme by computing its spectrum
* Implemented adjoint-state method to incorporate BFGS optimization to obtain optimal pulse
* Advisor: Dr. John Zweck
